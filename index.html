Your day counts

How each country counts days — rules checked

United Kingdom — tax year 6 April to 5 April. A day counts only if he is in the UK at midnight: the arrival day counts, the day he leaves doesn’t, and pure transit doesn’t. Under 16 days (if UK-resident in any of the last 3 tax years) or under 46 (if not) = automatically non-resident; 183 or more = automatically resident; in between, his UK ties decide (family, home, work, 90 days in either of the last 2 years, and for leavers, the country tie). Rare extras not tracked here: up to 60 days of exceptional circumstances can be ignored, and a “deeming” rule can count some departure days for people with 3+ ties and 30+ such days. HMRC RDR3

Schengen — 90 days in any rolling 180-day period, all Schengen countries together. Entry and exit days both count. Schengen Borders Code, Regulation (EU) 2016/399

Italy — calendar year. Resident if, for most of the year (183 days, or 184 in a leap year), he is physically present, registered, or has his habitual home or the centre of his personal and family life in Italy. Since 2024 any part of a day counts. Agenzia delle Entrate, Circular 20/E (2024)

Cyprus — calendar year. Resident with more than 183 days, or under the 60-day rule: 60+ days in Cyprus, no more than 183 days in any other single country, a Cyprus business, job or directorship, and a home in Cyprus. From 1 January 2026 he no longer has to be non-resident everywhere else. The departure day counts as outside Cyprus and the arrival day as inside; in and out on the same day counts as inside, out and back on the same day as outside. PwC Tax Summaries – Cyprus

United States — substantial presence test: at least 31 days in the current year and 183 days over three years, counting all days this year, a third of last year’s and a sixth of the year before. Any part of a day counts; transit under 24 hours between two places outside the US doesn’t. A closer-connection exception (Form 8840) can apply below 183 days in the current year. IRS

UAE — resident with 183 days in any 12 consecutive months, or 90 days with UAE/GCC nationality or a residence permit plus a home or a job/business in the UAE, or if the UAE is his main home and centre of interests. Any part of a day counts. Cabinet Decision 85/2022 (EY summary)

These counts are a guide for his tax adviser, who should confirm residence each year. A monthly check looks for rule changes; a change is applied from the date it starts, so earlier years keep the rule that applied then.

Or paste text — best for photos of bills

Most devices can copy the text out of a photo themselves, and read it better than this page can. Copy it, paste it here, and it’s read like a file. Works for any text: a bill, a hotel folio, an email.

  • iPhone / iPad: open the photo, press and hold on the text, Select All → Copy (Live Text, done on the device).
  • Mac: open the photo in Photos or Preview, select the text with the cursor, Copy (Live Text, done on the Mac).
  • Windows 11: Snipping Tool (Win+Shift+S), then Text actions → Copy all text.
  • Android: Google Lens or the phone’s “Select text” on the photo → Copy. Lens may send the photo to Google to read it, so for private documents prefer the camera button above, which reads on the phone.
